Design a complete modern responsive frontend UI/UX for a platform called:

TVET DIGITAL SKILLS ACADEMY

Tagline:
Learn. Build. Prove Your Skills.

The platform is an online learning and practical skills development ecosystem for TVET students. It focuses on three main learning tracks:

1. Software Development
2. Networking
3. Multimedia & Digital Design

The design must feel professional, modern, technical, trustworthy, youthful, and suitable for a national-level digital education platform in Rwanda.

IMPORTANT VISUAL DIRECTION:

Use the attached reference design as the main visual inspiration.

DO NOT copy the page exactly.

Instead, preserve its visual identity:
- Deep dark green backgrounds
- Bright green primary actions
- White content sections
- Terminal/code-inspired visual elements
- Monospace typography for technical labels
- Clean modern typography for normal content
- Thin borders
- Subtle shadows
- Minimal but sophisticated animations
- Strong spacing and large typography
- Professional developer/technology aesthetic

- Professional training academy
- Career development platform

- Generic school website designs

- Childish educational illustrations

Use subtle rounded corners, approximately 6–12px.
Keep the interface structured and spacious.
